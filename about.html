Perth / Kununurra Based

Practical drafting, product design, and 3D printing support for real-world jobs.

O.B & CO. combines technical drafting, functional design thinking, and fast-turnaround FDM production to help clients move from concept to usable part. The focus is on jobs that need to work in practice, not just look good on screen.

That includes drawing packages, custom parts, repair components, workshop tooling, prototype iterations, and short-run production pieces where speed and clear communication matter.

What We Do

  • Mechanical drafting and documentation support
  • HVAC drafting workflows and service coordination support
  • Functional CAD modelling for custom parts
  • 3D printed prototypes and small-batch production
  • Printability review and design-for-manufacture guidance

Technical First

Projects are approached from a usability and buildability perspective, with attention to dimensions, tolerance, strength, and practical assembly.

Fast Feedback

Instant quoting and direct communication help reduce back-and-forth when a part needs to be checked, revised, and produced quickly.

Flexible Scope

Support ranges from one-off parts and design tweaks through to repeatable short-run output for fixtures, housings, and replacement components.

How Work Typically Starts

Some jobs start with an existing STL or 3MF and move straight into pricing. Others start with a rough sketch, a broken part, a site condition, or a functional requirement that still needs drafting or modelling support. The site is structured to support both paths.

If you already have a printable file, go straight to the instant quote tool. If you need design or drafting help first, use the contact page to describe the job.
