3D Printing Pricing Guide

Use this guide to understand cost drivers before you upload. Your final instant quote is generated on the quote page using your exact settings.

Base Setup

Each unique part includes setup and machine prep time. Batch quantities lower effective per-part overhead.

Material Impact

PLA is the most cost-effective. PETG and ABS carry a higher multiplier due to print constraints and handling.

Quality & Infill

Fine layer height and higher infill increase print time and material use, which raises cost.

Turnaround

Priority and rush jobs apply schedule multipliers to reserve immediate machine capacity.
