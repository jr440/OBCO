Our Phone Assistant

Meet Steve

If Jesse can't get to the phone, you might be greeted by Steve, our AI phone assistant. Steve takes a quick message and passes it straight on to Jesse, so your call never goes unanswered.

Steve is an AI, not a person

How a Call Works

01

Steve Answers

When Jesse is busy, on site or catching up after hours, Steve picks up and lets you know he's an AI assistant.

02

Leave a Message

Steve asks for your name, what it's about and the best number to call you back on, then reads it back to check.

03

Jesse Follows Up

Your message goes straight to Jesse, and he'll get back to you as soon as he can.

What Steve Can & Can't Do

Steve Can

  • Answer when Jesse can't
  • Take your name, message and callback number
  • Read your message back so nothing gets missed
  • Pass it on to Jesse

Steve Can't

  • Give quotes, prices, dates or deadlines
  • Give technical advice or answer questions about jobs
  • Put your call through to Jesse
  • Share anyone's personal details, or details about other clients or projects

Common Questions

Am I talking to a real person?

No. Steve is an AI assistant and will always tell you so. He's there to take a message, not to replace Jesse.

When will Jesse call me back?

Steve can't promise a time, but your message goes straight to Jesse and he'll get back to you as soon as he can.

What happens to my message?

Your call is converted to text so Jesse can read your message. It's used only to follow up on your call and isn't shared with anyone else.

I'd rather not talk to an AI.

No problem. Just hang up and send Jesse a text, or email jesse@obandco.com.au.

Is it urgent?

Let Steve know it's urgent and why. Jesse will see that in your message.
