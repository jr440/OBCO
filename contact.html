What To Include

A stronger enquiry comes with enough context to tell whether you need drafting, design work, file fixes, or direct production.

Useful details

  • What the part, drawing, or design needs to do
  • Any key dimensions, tolerances, or material constraints
  • Whether you already have files, photos, or sketches
  • Your target deadline or whether the job is urgent
